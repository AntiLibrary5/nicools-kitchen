I remeber when I was at school, I used to be embarassed with my sandwich that was bigger than me. Bread, filled with Zwan delicacy and cucumber (Yes, I didn't have zankha receptors back then). Now, people look at zwan and simply pass by the isle, but not me, I'm a people pleaser, so I passed by zwan, bought it, cooked it, ate it, shat it. 

## Ingredients

* 1 small Hot zwan (with chilli, and yes it exists)
* 1 potato head
* 2 medium white onions
* 5 eggs
* Olive oil
* Salt 



## Instructions

1. Slice the onions
2. Cut the zwan into small cubes
3. Cut the potato into small cubes
4. In a pan, drizzle some olive oil and sauté the onions, zwan and potatoes until they're all cooked and golden brown
5. In a bowl, crack the eggs and whisk them with some salt and a bit of olive oil
6. Throw the eggs in the pan, on top of the zwan and potatoes and onions and cover the pan with a lid 
7. Once cooked on one side, you need to flip the omelette to cook in on the other side
8. Now focus with me: get a plat, ideally wider than the pan, and cover the pan with it.
9. Flip the omelette unto the plate, where the cooked side is facing GOD (if GOD exists for you) or facing the SKY (if the SKY exists for you)
10. Now slide the omelette back in the pan, and let it cook for w few minutes on the other side
