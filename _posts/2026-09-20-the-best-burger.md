## Ingredients

* Coarse Minced beef (70-30)
* Camembert cheese
* Fig Jam 
* 2 medium to big onions
* Brown sugar 
* Soy sauce
* Roasted sesame seeds
* French mayo (dijonaise)
* Moutarde a l'ancienne
* Brioche buns
* Salt and black pepper



## Instructions

1. Roll two big balls of your mince and then form them into a thick patty, we're making a gourmet burger here
2. Add salt and black pepper on both sides of the patty and leave it in room temperature
3. Slice your onions, and caramelize them with salt, olive oil, brown sugar and soy sauce
4. Once the onions are caramelized, add some roasted sesame seeds to them and leave them be 
5. Fire up your charcoal grill and toast both sides of your brioche buns 
6. Slice your camembert (I got mine from a cave in Zan "Cristoff Cheese", batroun, and they do really good cheese)
7. Lay your patties on the grill and cook them to your liking
8. Add the camembert while on the grill so all that cheesy goodness metls
9. Now assemble your burger: lower bun then mayo then grainy mustard > caramelized onions > beef patty , fig jam on the upper bun > close that burger 
10. Now go impress someone you like
