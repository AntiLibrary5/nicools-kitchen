I haven't posted a recipe in more than 4 months, so I figured I should go big or go home. So I went home and made myself the best pasta anyone can think of, with ingredients everyone has on hands: Bird's yogurt, horse tail, and few other stuff you can find in any supermarket on Planet Mars.

## Ingredients

* 3 medium (to small) zucchinis
* 1 whole lemon 
* 2 to 3 garlic cloves
* Sea salt staright from Nose in North Lebanon
* Enormous amounts of Black pepper
* Grated Parmesan cheese
* 1/4 tspn chilli flakes
* Very extra virgin olive oil 
* 200g of MY HOMEMADE ricotta cheese
* 75 ML of cooking cream
* 300g to 400g of any pasta shape besides spaghetti



## Instructions

### For the Ricotta cheese
1. Go back to the home Page 
2. Click on the "Add Ons" button
3. Click on the ricotta cheese recipe
4. Read it
5. Do it 

### For everything else
1. You great person, go grate the zucchinis, season them with some salt and then set them aside in a bowl
2. Do you have a crush? Same sis, now go crush your garlic
4. Boil some sea salt water, which is just water that you need to salt 
5. Before cooking your zucchinis, remove them from the bowl and press them down to remove their water content (don't skip this step like you skipped your meal last night)
6. Heat up some olive oil in a pan, and then add the zucchinis and the crushed cloves and let them sweat together
7. While your pasta and your zucchinis are cooking, start making THE sauce
8. In a bowl, mix together your ricotta, a lot of olive oil, chilli flakes, huge amount of freshly cracked black pepper, juice of the whole lemon, zest of the lemon you just juiced, parmesan, salt, and your cooking cream 
9. Mix them as if your life depends on it (at this point, I think my like really depends on it)
10. Once the zucchinis and garlic are cooked, mix them well with the ricotta mixture 
11. Drain your pasta, but think of leaving some pasta water 
12. Add the pasta water, that you thought of leaving before you drained your pasta, to the now zucchini and ricotta mixture
13. Mix for a last Time
14. Now add the pasta, mix for a last time also
15. Add a drizzle of olive oil and serve yourself
