## Ingredients

* 600g of boneless skinless chicken thighs
* 1.5 cups of WASHED basmati rice 
* 1 cup chicken stock 
* 1 cup coconut milk
* 3 Garlic cloves
* 1 onion
* Grated ginger equivalent to the size of your big toe
* 1 tablespoon of honey, honey
* Olive oil
* Salt
* Black Pepper
* Chilli Flakes



## Instructions

1. Slice, dice, smah, crash, spit, kill the onions, garlic, and ginger, and set aside
2. Heavily season the chickens with salt and black pepper 
3. Heat up your pot to the roof and place the chicken till they char on one side (should be around 2 to 3 minutes
4. Flip the chickens and let them char on the other side 
5. Once the thighs are burnt, remove them from the pot
6. Drizzle a bit of olive oil
7. Add the diced onions, garlic and ginger and let them cook 
8. Add salt, black pepper and chilli Flakes
9. Honey, bring that honey and add it
10. Cook for a few minutes until the onions are soft
11. Add the washed rice and cook with the veggies for few minutes 
12. Throw it the stock and the coconut milk
13. Add back the chicken
14. Cover the pot and wait for 20 minutes 
15. Should be OK I guess
