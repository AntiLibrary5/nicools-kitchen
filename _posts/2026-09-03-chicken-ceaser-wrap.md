## Ingredients

* BLT but remove the B and the T
* 1 cup Khoury greek Yogurt
* 1 tbsp Mayo
* 1 tspn capers
* 1 small garlic clove
* 1 tspn dijon mustard
* Worshshshshshshester sauce
* Salt and BP
* A lot, like really a lot of grated parm
* The japanese bread crumbs...mmmm.....Panco!
* Durum know to some of you by Tortilla wrap
* 200g of boneless, skinless, lifeless chicken thighs



## Instructions

1. Girl, for the thighs, please do the effort of checking my chicken thighs recipe. 
2. The L in BLT stands for lettuce, in case you're still wondering. Go grab those Ls. 
3. Wash the lettuce until no more insects live inside
4. DON't shred the lettuce
5. For the dressing mix all the above ingredients together, except the obvious YOU and ME. Joking! chicken and lettuce, duh
6. Oh and also don't mix the panco with the dressing
7. Heat up a pan with some olive oil, and brown the panco until golden brown. Season with a bit of salt, black pepper and some parmesan cheese
8. On a cutting board, place the lettuce papers, put on top the chicken and the dressing and start shredding until they become like prison food
9. Get the tortilla wrap, but the mixture inside, add the breadcrumbs on top, some parm and wrap it tight! 
10. Enjoy it while it lasts, girl
