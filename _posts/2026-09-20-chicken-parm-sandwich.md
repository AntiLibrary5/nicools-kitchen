## Ingredients

* 1 ciabatta bread
* 1 tablespoom mayo
* 2 small garlic cloves 
* 1 Chicken breast
* A lot of mozarella cheese
* Panco bread crumbs
* 2 eggs
* Some flour for coating
* Parmesan Cheese
* Tomato Puree
* Salt
* Black pepper
* Dried Basil
* Olive Oil


## Instructions

1. Girl, wake up, go to work (if you do work), then after work, pass by the supermarket, get the above list and head back home. 
2. Get your keys, open the door to your house, remove your shoes, wear the best socks you have and head to the kitchen. 
3. Start by cutting two flat slices of your chicken breast, this big and this thin. Make sure they're evenly thick. Do your thing, I don't know how to explain this step. 
4. Crack a joke, you're funny girl, then crack two eggs and whisk them with a pinch of salt.
5. In another plate, throw in some flour, and get yourself a flower, if you like flowers, and if you don't then don't. 
6. In a third plate, yes be ready for a lot of cleaning afterward, throw in some panco, a bit of salt, black pepper, and freshly grate a generous amount of parmesan cheese. 
7. Now, you're about to get dirty, take your chicken, 1 by 1, coat in the flour, then throw it in the egg for a wash, and then coat well in the panco. Repeat. 
8. Put on a rack and let it sit for a bit because you need to move to the next step. 
9. Now slice open your bread, drizzle some olive oil and toast it really well 
10. Drench your pan with olive oil, and deep fry the two garlic cloves until they're soft.
11. Once your garlic is cooked, smash it and mix it with the mayo wil a pinch of salt. 
12. In the same oil you used to cook the garlic in, add the tomato puree, salt, black pepper, dried basil and it cook until thick 
13. Now for the hard part, frying the chicken. 
14. In another pan, throw in some neutral oil, heat it up, and then throw in the chicken. Fry on each side for 2 minutes ONLY for medium rare cuisson
15. Jesus, this is becoming long and boring and I'm not liking this a allll
16. Anw, in an oven dish, place your fried chicken, then place some marinara on top, and cover the whole thing with mozarella cheese and bake until cheese it melted and golden 
17. Finally, for the assemble, get the toasted bread, layer up some garlic mayo, lay up your two chicken breasts so they overlap, and cover your sandwich 
18. Now, enjoy while you can because your sink is sinking, you'll need to remove the oil your sprayed everywhere
